Someone is pounding on your neighbor's door.
Then the pounding stops.
And the screaming starts.

Lock the door, and push the couch against it.
Locks stop people. Weight stops crowds.

Two.

A full tub holds about a hundred and fifty liters.
Boil it, and that's a month of drinking water.

Three.
Kill the lights, and cover the windows.
At night, one glowing window tells the whole street.
Food here.

Four.
Charge everything. Phone, power bank, flashlight.
The grid is next.

Five.
Eat the fridge first.
Without power, it stays cold for about four hours.
The cans can wait.

By midnight, the hallway is quiet.
Then your phone lights up. It's your neighbor.
